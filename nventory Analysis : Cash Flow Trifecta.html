Inventory Analysis / Cash Flow Trifecta

Three measures that can be handled together as they all affect cash flow. They use the 80/20 rule to guide where to start or focus your efforts.

📊
The 80/20 rule applies to all three: 80% of the value in your stock sits in 20% of items. 80% of your overdue accounts come from 20% of your debtors. 80% of your credit risk comes from 20% of your customers. Start with that 20% — always.
