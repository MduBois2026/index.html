Reducing costs (or controlling costs that have a tendency to blow out) has the same effect as increasing prices — you can make better margin from doing exactly the same work. Reducing costs is not the same as "being tight" or "buying cheap". If either of these affect the customer experience negatively then it will be a false economy.
