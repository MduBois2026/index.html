Discount Policy

Discounting is the single biggest killer of margin — it happens in seconds at point of sale: "how about I do it for…" or "I'll match it". Working on sales strategies to use in front of customers will assist with this bad habit. Discounts can also be part of old agreements that have long since lost relevance — such as discounts for on-time payments that are never on time, or when a customer used to buy volume and now buys a lot less, or a customer qualified for a discount simply because they were a certain type of customer.
