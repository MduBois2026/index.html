Chargeable Hours

Optimising the "direct labour" hours worked relative to the hours that end up on an invoice — your recovery rate. Divide your measure in Column 2 into Column 1. Higher is better. Every unrecovered hour has a direct dollar cost.

Example: 5 staff × 40 hrs/week = 200 payroll hours. At 80% recovery = 160 chargeable hours. If actual recovery is only 60%, you're losing 40 hours/week = 1 full-timer worth of revenue. At $50/hr that's $100,000 per year walking out the door.
